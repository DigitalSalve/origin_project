# Legal Documents

**Purpose:** Single location for legal and compliance documents (terms of service, privacy policy, cookie policy, disclaimers, etc.). These are the **source-of-truth copies** used for review, versioning, and deployment to the app or website.

**Location:** `docs/06-operations/legal/`

**Management:** Follow [Legal Documents SOP](../LEGAL_DOCUMENTS_SOP.md) when adding, updating, or retiring legal documents.

**When to use:** Before creating or changing any legal document; when publishing or linking legal text in the app or website; when responding to legal or compliance requests.

**Documents in this folder:**
- [COOKIE_POLICY.md](COOKIE_POLICY.md) — **User-facing** cookie and tracking disclosure (what we tell users about cookies, purposes, retention, choices). Standard-compliant (GDPR/ePrivacy); aligned with technical usage in [Cookies and Session](../../04-behavior/COOKIES_AND_SESSION.md).

**Typical files (add per project):**
- `TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md` — Terms of service / terms of use
- `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` — Privacy policy
- `DISCLAIMERS.md` — Product or campaign disclaimers (if applicable)
- Other project-specific legal docs (e.g. `ACCESSIBILITY.md`, `CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md`)

**Naming:** Use `UPPER_SNAKE_CASE.md` for document names. Keep one file per document type; use versioning or dated archives only if the project explicitly requires it (see Legal Documents SOP).
